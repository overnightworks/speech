laughter.wav is the Freesound clip "Girl Laughing.wav" by jgreer,
https://freesound.org/people/jgreer/sounds/335581/, licence CC0 1.0
(http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as FSD50K ships it
(evaluation clip 335581, labels Giggle and Laughter; FSD50K is itself CC-BY 4.0
as a collection, each clip keeps its own licence).

It is converted from the source's 44.1 kHz mono PCM16 to 16 kHz mono PCM16 and
otherwise unchanged. scripts/build_sound_model.py holds the ONNX to the PyTorch
model on every 2 s window of it, hopped by 0.5 s.
